# Routines

A Routine is something an agent or a person does again and again, step
by step: its goal, its procedure and when it is done.

`routines/<slug>/ROUTINE.md`.

## Fields

Besides the [common fields](/format/documents#frontmatter-of-a-piece):

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `type` | Yes | `Routine`. No `kind`. |
| `steps` | No | The procedure, as Markdown. |
| `definition_of_done` | No | How to tell it is done. |

The body is the routine's **goal**.

Links: `used_by` toward the [roles](/format/roles) that run it, and
`uses` toward the [channels](/format/channels) it works through. The
[objectives](/format/objectives) it moves and the
[connections](/format/connections) it needs point at it from their side.

```yaml filename="routines/calificar-consulta-mayorista/ROUTINE.md"
---
type: Routine
title: Calificar consulta mayorista
name: calificar-consulta-mayorista
definition_of_done: La conversación tiene respuesta, etiqueta de tipo y, si es pedido, su deal en Actividades asociado al contacto.
steps: |-
  1. Al despertar por un mensaje entrante, leé la conversación completa en WhatsApp.
  2. Aplicá la skill `calificar-consulta-mayorista`.
  3. Respondé por WhatsApp con la conexión del canal; registrá el deal en el tablero Mayorista si hay intención de compra.
  4. Dejá en la conversación la nota con lo hecho.
links:
  - role: used_by
    to: roles/mayorista/atencion
    created_at: 2026-10-15T15:47:33.367Z
  - role: uses
    to: channels/whatsapp-mayorista
    created_at: 2026-10-15T15:47:33.367Z
created_at: 2026-10-15T15:47:33.367Z
updated_at: 2026-10-16T11:26:47.927Z
---
Responder y calificar cada consulta mayorista que entra por WhatsApp en
menos de 30 minutos, dejando el deal registrado cuando corresponde.
```
