# Routines

A Routine is something an agent or a person does again and again, step
by step: its goal, its procedure and when it is done.

`routines/<slug>/ROUTINE.md`.

## Fields

Besides the [common fields](/format/documents#frontmatter-of-a-piece):

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `type` | Yes | `Routine`. No `kind`. |
| `steps` | No | The procedure, as Markdown. |
| `definition_of_done` | No | How to tell it is done. |

The body is the routine's **goal**.

Links: `used_by` toward the [roles](/format/roles) that run it, and
`uses` toward the [channels](/format/channels) it works through. The
[OKRs](/format/okrs) it moves and the
[connections](/format/connections) it needs point at it from their side.

```yaml filename="routines/seguimiento-de-consultas/ROUTINE.md"
---
type: Routine
title: Seguimiento de consultas
name: seguimiento-de-consultas
definition_of_done: Toda consulta del día tiene respuesta y próximo paso.
steps: |-
  1. Revisá las consultas sin respuesta de las últimas 24 horas.
  2. Respondé o derivá cada una.
  3. Agendá el próximo contacto.
links:
  - role: used_by
    to: roles/ventas/vendedor
    created_at: 2026-10-18T15:35:05.163Z
  - role: uses
    to: channels/whatsapp-ventas
    created_at: 2026-10-18T15:35:05.163Z
created_at: 2026-10-18T15:35:05.163Z
updated_at: 2026-10-18T15:35:05.163Z
created_by: user:equipo@acme.example
updated_by: user:equipo@acme.example
---
Que ninguna consulta quede sin respuesta ni sin seguimiento.
```
