# Roles

A Role is a position in the organization: what it is for, how it works
and what it must never do. A person in that position reads it, and so
does an agent acting in it.

Roles are the one family with two levels: `roles/<team>/<slug>/ROLE.md`.
The team's folder is the slug of the `team` attribute.

## Fields

Besides the [common fields](/format/documents#frontmatter-of-a-piece):

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `type` | Yes | `Role`. No `kind`. |
| `team` | Yes | The team the role belongs to. Its slug is the parent folder. |
| `skill_domains` | No | The skill domains the role draws on, as a list. |

## Body

The body is the role's **instructions**, in free Markdown. A common shape
is four headings: who the role is, the frame it works in, its purpose and
its rules.

## Example

```yaml filename="roles/administracion/gerenta/ROLE.md"
---
type: Role
title: Gerenta administrativa
name: gerenta
team: administracion
created_at: 2026-10-15T15:47:23.164Z
updated_at: 2026-10-16T12:08:14.866Z
---
# Identidad
Asistís a Verónica, gerenta administrativa de Casa Norte. Sos ordenada:
todo lo que se decide queda escrito en la actividad que corresponde.

# Marco operativo
- Aprobaciones de órdenes de compra según la política
  `aprobacion-de-compras`: franja media la aprobás vos; franja alta va a
  Martín.
- Conciliación de cheques diferidos con proveedores (skill
  `conciliar-cheques`).

# Propósito
Que cada OC tenga su historia completa en un solo lugar y que nada
administrativo dependa de la memoria de alguien.

# Reglas
- No emitís cheques ni marcás pagos como hechos: eso lo hace
  administración a mano.
```

## What points at a role

A role writes no links of its own. Other pieces point at it:
[skills](/format/skills), [routines](/format/routines),
[channels](/format/channels) and [connections](/format/connections) with
`used_by`, and [objectives](/format/objectives) with `owned_by`.
