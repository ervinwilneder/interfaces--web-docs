# Reading and importing

What a reader checks, and what an importer does, so that a bundle
written by one system reads the same in another.

## Checks

A reader rejects a bundle, saying which file and why, when:

- `POF.md` is missing, or its `type`, `format` and `revision` are not
  `Bundle`, `pof` and a revision it reads. A reader of one revision is
  not required to read the others.
- A file outside `extensions/` is not part of the
  [layout](/format#layout).
- A piece's `type` does not match its folder (`policies/…/POLICY.md`
  with `type: Role`), or its `name` does not match its folder's slug.
- A piece has no `title`, or a slug is not valid.
- A `kind` is not one of its family's.
- An attribute does not hold against its type: a required one is
  missing, or a value has the wrong shape.
- A subentity's `type` does not match its folder, or it sits in a folder
  its family does not have (`rules/` under an OKR).
- A link's `role` is not one of the [five roles](/format/documents#links), or its `to` is not a piece of the bundle.
- A `HISTORY.md` comes without `COMMITS.md`, or names a commit, a
  subentity or a link that is not there.

An importer checks everything before writing anything: a bundle is
taken whole or not at all.

## Import

- **Identity by folder.** The importer gives each piece its own ids;
  what identifies a piece is its folder, and links are resolved from
  paths.
- **With history**, it keeps commit ids, dates and actors, so the
  organization arrives with its past.
- **Without history**, the bundle is a creation: one commit by whoever
  imports it, a `create` for every piece, subentity and link.
- **Extensions** it does not know stay as they are. It does not read
  them, drop them or rewrite them, binaries included.
- **A whole bundle**, with the core and several extensions, is imported
  part by part: each system takes its own folder and resolves the `/…`
  paths against the root of the bundle. A path that is not in the bundle
  is rejected, with the file and the path. An extension imported on its
  own, outside a bundle, keeps its `/…` paths unchecked.
- **Access** is not imported because it is not there: keys, credentials
  and connections' secrets are set up again by whoever imports.

## Writing

A writer produces the same bundle for the same content: keys in a
stable order, subentities in their order, the manifest dated by the
last commit rather than by the moment of export. Neither the bundle nor
the manifests of its extensions carry the client's name or the date of
the export; they do carry `as_of`, the date of their content, which is
the date of its last commit. That is why an export, an import and
another export give the same bytes, and a diff between two exports
shows exactly what changed.
