# Channels

A Channel is a place where the organization talks to someone: a WhatsApp
line, a Slack workspace, a Telegram bot, an inbox.

`channels/<slug>/CHANNEL.md`.

## Fields

Besides the [common fields](/format/documents#frontmatter-of-a-piece):

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `type` | Yes | `Channel`. |
| `kind` | Yes | The platform: `slack`, `discord`, `teams`, `telegram`, `twilio`, `github`, `linear`, `chat_sdk`, `mcp`, `linq`, `photon`, `http`, `custom` or `other`. |
| `identifier` | No | How the channel is addressed on its platform: a number, a handle, a workspace. |
| `platform_name` | No | The platform's name, for `custom` and `other`. |

The body is the channel's **description**.

Links: `used_by` toward the [roles](/format/roles) that work through it.

```yaml filename="channels/whatsapp-ventas/CHANNEL.md"
---
type: Channel
kind: other
title: WhatsApp de ventas
name: whatsapp-ventas
platform_name: WhatsApp
links:
  - role: used_by
    to: roles/ventas/vendedor
    created_at: 2026-10-18T15:25:01.623Z
created_at: 2026-10-18T15:25:01.623Z
updated_at: 2026-10-18T15:25:01.623Z
created_by: user:equipo@acme.example
updated_by: user:equipo@acme.example
---
El número donde entran las consultas de clientes nuevos.
```
